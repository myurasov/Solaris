# kaggle 0.11.0 - The Master as an Autocode Agent, the Hourly Wake's Check <!-- omit in toc -->

For packs updating from 0.10.0. The plugin now requires the `autocode` plugin: a Kaggle project's master runs only
as an autocode agent, an OpenCode 2 session, and the session clock, the hook runs and `solaris.tools.ai_spend` are no
longer what the plugin's tools read. What a project changes:

- The hourly pass is the check of the master's `hourly` wake, given to the wake as the guard `sh
  <pack>/plugins/kaggle/tools/kaggle_hourly_check.sh <slug>`: a quiet hour takes no turn, a woken hour ends with
  `kaggle_hourly.py <slug> --close`, and a wake that is not closed wakes again every hour.
- The pack names the competition's hosts for the hourly check, which otherwise wakes for every new comment.
- Outside reviews run through `kaggle_review.py`, and review jobs are kept as files in `<pack>/jobs/`.
- A wait for a score is a watched job that runs `kaggle_watch.py`, in place of a hand-made poll script.
- The owner retired direction OD03 (a master turn at least every 55 minutes, for a warm prompt cache) on 2026-11-22,
  and `ODnn` numbers now belong to the plugin: a project numbers its own directions `L01`, `L02`, ....

**Who carries it out.** Steps 1 and 2 change the pack's managed copies: the owner, or the session that manages the
update, runs them at the Solaris root. The other steps are read and carried out by the project's own master session,
in a turn of its own, at a quiet point: never during a submit, a score read, a kernel push or a status page build,
and not in the hour before the daily reset. Nothing here writes to Kaggle.

## Moved Paths

None: no project file or log moves. What is new, and what is no longer read:

- **New in the copy:** `tools/kaggle_hourly_check.sh`, `tools/kaggle_watch.py`, `tools/kaggle_review.py`,
  `tools/kaggle_jobs.py`, `tools/kaggle_scout.py`, `jobs/plan-review.json`, `kaggle-reviews.skill.md` and
  `kaggle-scout.skill.md`.
- **New state, all local and out of git:**
  - `__data/kaggle/<slug>/hourly/<YYYYMMDDTHH>Z.json` (the check's receipts, kept 30 days), `run.lock`,
    `status-render.lock` and `status-build.log` beside `last.json`, which gains keys and reads an older one as it
    is;
  - `__data/kaggle/<slug>/watch/<name>.json` (a watch's heartbeat);
  - `__data/kaggle/review/` (the passed key gates, the gate calls' output and the records of runs in progress) and
    `__out/review/<ID>/` (each review with its record);
  - `<pack>/jobs/<name>.json` (job definitions; these are pack files, kept in git).
- **The leaderboard store:** `index.jsonl` lines gain `"unreliable"`, and a snapshot whose ranks cannot be trusted
  has `-unreliable` in its file name. Older snapshots keep their names.
- **No longer read:** `<pack>/.memory/clock.json` and this project's own `<pack>/.memory/alerts.jsonl`, by the
  hourly pass; `solaris.tools.ai_spend`, by the hourly pass and by the status page. The `agents` and `spend` lines
  and the status page's AI spend read `autocode me --json`, and `peers` reads each peer's
  `<pack>/.memory/guard/check.json`.
- **A review runner of the project's own** kept other names. Where it wrote `ctx_files.txt`, the plugin's runner
  writes `copies.txt`. There is no OpenCode home to keep in the project any more: each run makes its own in a
  temporary folder and removes it. Usage and latency logs of its own (a `usage.jsonl`, a sessions list in
  `<pack>/.memory/`) have no successor: `run.json` beside each review and the cost ledger line replace them.

## Pre-flight

- The project has this plugin copied at `<pack>/plugins/kaggle/` (`<pack>` is the project's ai-pack folder: default
  `aipack/`, `ai/` in older projects, any name). A linked project runs the live source: it skips step 2, does the
  rest, and names the tools where its link file points (`shared/tools/` under the plugin root).
- The master runs as an autocode agent: `autocode_me action=status` answers and names it. A project whose master
  still runs under a session clock in another harness moves first: attach autocode (step 1), then start the session
  with `<pack>/plugins/autocode/autocode-start.skill.md`. This file assumes that move is done.
- The point is quiet. No submission record holds an attempt that is `submitting`, no kernel push is under way, and
  `python3 <pack>/plugins/autocode/tools/guard.py job list` shows no watch about to end.
- List what names the old ways, and keep the list for Validate (skip what does not exist):

  ```sh
  grep -rnE "session_clock|clock\.json|cap 55|ai_spend|alerts\.jsonl|prompt cache|OD[0-9]+|kaggle_hourly|sleep" \
    tools/ <pack>/instructions.md <pack>/card.md <pack>/.memory/context.md <pack>/.memory/schedule.json
  ```

  Then `autocode_wake action=list` (the `hourly` wake and its check, any wake made to watch a score or to keep a
  turn coming every 55 minutes) and `autocode_me action=jobs` (the review jobs).
- Know what happens by itself (step 12): the first gateway call rebuilds `.venv-kaggle`, and the first leaderboard
  read upgrades the index. Step 12 has both happen now, not inside the first hourly check, which has a time limit.

## Migrate

1. **Attach autocode** where the pack lacks it, at the Solaris root: "install plugin autocode to `<project>`". It
   comes first because `plugins check` and the update of step 2 stop without it. A pack that has an older autocode
   updates it in the same go; its own steps are in `plugins/autocode/migrations/0.5.0.md`.
2. **Update the copy,** at the Solaris root: the `update-project` skill when the framework release this version
   comes with has a migration of its own (Solaris 0.49.0 has one); for a later patch release `uv run -m
   solaris.tools.version update --dir projects/<slug>` (with `--dry-run` first; exit 3 names what stops it, a
   framework migration on the way among them); or `install-plugin` update. Every `shared/` file of 0.11.0 lands
   in `<pack>/plugins/kaggle/`, the entry `{"name": "kaggle", "version": "0.11.0"}` in `<pack>/manifest.json`, and
   the `revisions` entries are baselined. Fold any project-side edit to a plugin file into the plugin first
   (`import-plugin`). The steps from here on are the master's.
3. **Read again** in the session, since the copies it loaded before are stale: `kaggle.rule.md`, the playbook core
   `how-to-kaggle.skill.md`, `kaggle-checks.skill.md` and `kaggle-reviews.skill.md`.
4. **Pack text.** In `<pack>/instructions.md`, with the type's overlay as the model
   (`plugins/kaggle/project-types/competition/{{PACK}}/instructions.append.md` in the Solaris checkout):
   - replace the "Session clock" line with the overlay's "Wakes" line;
   - add the `## Autocode` section if it is missing: the agent ID, the start prompt, and this project's wakes with
     their triggers and checks;
   - reword the "Hourly pass", "Status page", "Spend" and "Cross-family review" lines as the overlay has them:
     the check is the guard, a woken hour ends with `--close`, and the page is not rebuilt by hand at each pass;
   - add the "Direction IDs" line;
   - reword the "Hard-rules card" line: the master reads the card again after each compaction (the autocode Start
     skill's Read step).

   In `<pack>/card.md`, drop "arm the session clock before every turn end". Then refresh the table of contents of
   `<pack>/instructions.md`.
5. **Direction numbers.** `ODnn`, `Cn` and `Dn` numbers are the plugin's registry. List the plugin's own with `grep
   -oh "OD[0-9]*" <pack>/plugins/kaggle/*.md | sort -u`: an `ODnn` the project uses that is not in that list was
   minted by the project. Renumber each with an `L` prefix (`L01`, `L02`, ...) in `<pack>/.memory/directions.md`,
   `<pack>/card.md`, `<pack>/instructions.md` and every note or script that cites it, and keep one line in the
   directions log that maps each old number to its new one. **OD03 is retired:** drop every line that owes a master
   turn every 55 minutes or keeps a prompt cache warm, and cancel a wake that was set only for that. Keep a wake of
   30 minutes or less without a check where it keeps the project's folder loaded on a machine that is not the
   autocode server's own (the autocode Start skill, Where the Session Comes Back by Itself): that one is not OD03.
6. **The hourly wake.** Set it again (the same name replaces the wake), with its trigger as it is:

   ```text
   autocode_wake action=set name=hourly trigger=<as before>
     check="sh <pack>/plugins/kaggle/tools/kaggle_hourly_check.sh <slug>" check_timeout=5m
     text="The hourly Kaggle check found something: its reasons and receipt follow. Act on each from the receipt,
           under __data/kaggle/<slug>/hourly/ (make no second pass), then run python3
           <pack>/plugins/kaggle/tools/kaggle_hourly.py <slug> --close, read what it prints, and paste its STATUS
           block into the status message. Fired by hand, the check did not run: run sh
           <pack>/plugins/kaggle/tools/kaggle_hourly_check.sh <slug> once."
   ```

   The check is the guard script, never `python3 .../kaggle_hourly.py <slug> --check` itself: Python exits 1,
   with no output, for a tool file that does not compile and for an interpreter that does not start, and autocode
   would read that as a quiet hour. The guard lets exit 1 through only for a verified quiet pass. Arguments after
   the slug go to `--check` (`--vs <score>`, `--limit <seconds>`).

   Record the wake and its check under "Autocode" in `<pack>/instructions.md`. From now on, close each woken hour
   with `python3 <pack>/plugins/kaggle/tools/kaggle_hourly.py <slug> --close`: it rebuilds the status page offline,
   marks handled every receipt since the last close, prints in full the reasons and all the held news they leave,
   then the STATUS block. Read that list at every close. A wake that is not closed wakes again every hour until
   it is, so a forgotten close costs a turn an hour. A rebuild of the page by hand takes the close's lock: `flock
   __data/kaggle/<slug>/hourly/status-render.lock python3 <pack>/plugins/kaggle/tools/kaggle_status.py --keep-rev`.
7. **A check of the project's own.** A project that wrapped the hourly pass in a check script of its own keeps only
   the parts that know more than the plugin's: its own job, queue and host checks. Its Kaggle reads, its receipts
   and its rules for what wakes go, since the plugin's check now has them. The wrapper runs the plugin's guard
   and the project's own check beside it, prints the plugin's output first (autocode quotes the first 2000
   characters), and exits 0 when either wakes:

   ```sh
   #!/bin/sh
   # the hourly wake's check: the plugin's guard, then this project's own check; exit 1 only when both are quiet
   sh <pack>/plugins/kaggle/tools/kaggle_hourly_check.sh <slug>; kaggle=$?
   out=$(python3 tools/<the project's own check>); own=$?
   [ -n "$out" ] && printf '%s\n' "$out"
   case "$own:$out" in 1:quiet:*) ;; *) own=0 ;; esac
   [ "$kaggle" = 1 ] && [ "$own" = 1 ] && exit 1
   exit 0
   ```

   The own part follows the guard's rule: it counts as quiet only when it exits 1 and its output starts with
   `quiet:`, since a script that does not compile also exits 1, with no output. So the project's check prints such
   a line when, and only when, it is sure that nothing needs the master, and gives the reason otherwise. It must be
   safe to run again and while a turn runs. Give the wake a `check_timeout` that covers both parts (the plugin's
   takes up to its `--limit`, 240 seconds by default, and the guard allows 20 seconds more; the owner's server sets
   the most a check may take, 10 minutes by default).
8. **The hosts, and a first review.** The forum read carries no author type, so the hourly check tells a host's
   comment only by name. Name the competition's hosts in `__data/kaggle/<slug>/presubmit/config.json`: `{"hosts":
   ["<display name>", ...], "host_topics": [<topic id>, ...]}` (the display names as the forum shows them). The
   hourly check goes by the `hosts` list alone: `host_topics` serves the pre-submit check and does not stop
   comments from waking. Until `hosts` names them, every new comment in a known topic wakes the master, since
   any of them may be a host's. With hosts named, a named host's comment wakes and the others are held for the
   next wake and the close. A project with no recorded pre-submit review also runs
   `kaggle_presubmit.py <slug>` once, reads it and `--ack`s it: the check tells a competition page change only
   after a first review is recorded.
9. **Settings,** in `<pack>/.memory/config.json` (or `<pack>/defaults.json`):
   - `"kaggle.wake_line_move"`. Its default stays any move (0), and a board over 1,000 teams needs a value: there
     the places of the medal lines shift as teams join, so a line's score moves a little in most hours, and with
     the default most hours wake. Set the smallest move, in score units, that would change a decision.
   - `"kaggle.wake_on"`, to taste: the held kinds that wake as well, a list of `"rank"`, `"top"`, `"comments"`,
     `"notebooks"` and `"status"`, or `"all"`. Without it they are held for the next wake and the close.
   - `"kaggle.board_jump"`, where the project passed `--jump` to the pass: the check takes it from here.
   - `"kaggle.allowed_models"`: the list must name the master's model as autocode names it, or the `agents` line
     flags it. Run the plain pass once (`kaggle_hourly.py <slug>`) and read the line: `master <id> on <model>
     (<display name>), effort <level>; workers: not read`. A model matches by its id (`<provider>/<model>`), its id
     without the provider, its family (`claude-opus-5-5` inside a longer id) or its display name ("Opus 5.5", also
     as `claude-opus-5-5`). The default list is `claude-opus-5-5`, `claude-sonnet-5-5`, `claude-haiku-*`. Where the
     owner chose another model for the master, add its id or a pattern (`"<provider>/<model>"`, `"claude-<family>-*"`);
     a model the owner did not choose stays a flag to tell the owner.
   - `"kaggle.master_effort"`, where the pack sets it: its value is the level's name as autocode gives it (the
     `agents` line prints it).
10. **Reviews.** Move outside reviews to the plugin's runner (`kaggle-reviews.skill.md`):
    - Name the reviewers in the pack: `"kaggle.review.model"` and `"kaggle.review.variant"` for one, or
      `"kaggle.review.pool"` (`{"<name>": {"model": "<provider/model>", "variant": "<variant>"}, ...}`) for several.
      Where needed: `"kaggle.review.timeout"`, `"kaggle.review.max_steps"` (the most model calls one review may
      make, 150 by default: with the time limit it is what bounds a review's cost, since there is no limit in
      dollars), `"kaggle.review.base_url"`, `"kaggle.review.category"`, `"kaggle.review.max_mb"`,
      `"kaggle.review.session_config"`.
    - The key gate first, which costs nothing: `python3 <pack>/plugins/kaggle/tools/kaggle_review.py --gate --model
      <provider/model>` must exit 0 for each reviewer. Exit 6 is a stop: tell the owner, and do not work around it.
    - **Run one small real review before relying on it daily:** a prompt of a few questions and one short file,
      `python3 <pack>/plugins/kaggle/tools/kaggle_review.py <ID> <prompt file> <file> --model <provider/model>`. It
      is a paid call on the session's own key, a small fraction of a daily review's cost, and its exact cost is the
      line it adds to `<pack>/.memory/spend.jsonl`. Read `review.md` and `run.json` (no path outside the folder, the
      denied calls) before the reviewer joins the daily pool. A review can take as long as its `--timeout` (an hour
      by default): called from a turn, give the shell tool a time limit above it, or run it as a watched job.
    - A project with a runner of its own retires it once that review passed. The plugin's runner reads the session's
      own key, so no key file or environment prefix of the project's is needed any more; the names that changed are
      under Moved Paths. The runner keeps no OpenCode home: each run has its own, in a temporary folder that is
      removed afterwards, so a home the project kept for its runner can be pruned by its housekeeping rules. A
      package folder used again every day now works: the day's `READY` makes it a new package, and the earlier
      review keeps a dated name beside the new one.
    - Keep the review job definitions in `<pack>/jobs/`. Before any change to a job, its current definition must be
      in the pack: ask the owner, or a manager session with the owner's CLI, to run `python3
      <pack>/plugins/kaggle/tools/kaggle_jobs.py backup` once (in an agent's shell it stops with exit 3). Then make
      each job from the template, one per reviewer: `kaggle_jobs.py new review-<name> --template plan-review --set
      REVIEWER=<name> --set REVIEW_MODEL=<provider/model> --set JOB_MODEL=<provider/model> --set
      PACKAGE_FOLDER=<folder>`. `JOB_MODEL` is the model of the job's own session, which only starts the runner: a
      cheap model the pack allows, not the reviewer's. `new` fills in the folder of the plugin's tools and the
      trigger's zone from the pack's `"owner.timezone"` (else add `--set ZONE=<zone>`). The trigger is 07:00 every
      day in that zone: change the hour in the file to the one the package is ready by. Set the job with the
      `autocode_me` tool from `kaggle_jobs.py show review-<name> --tool` (the owner may have to approve it).
    - The daily review is these jobs, each with a cron trigger of its own. A `plan-review` wake, where the project
      has one, is only the master's turn to write the day's package and `READY` before the jobs' hour: reword its
      text so and set it before that hour, or cancel a wake that ran the reviews itself.
11. **Waits for scores.** Replace hand-made poll scripts (a loop with `sleep` that ends in `autocode fire me`, a
    background poller, a wake set only to look at the submission list) with a watched job. Set the wake first,
    `autocode_wake action=set name=<job>-done trigger="fire-only once" text=<what to do with the score>`, then:

    ```sh
    python3 <pack>/plugins/autocode/tools/guard.py job start --name <job> --wake <job>-done --deadline <d> -- \
        python3 <pack>/plugins/kaggle/tools/kaggle_watch.py <slug> --ref <ref> --limit <minutes>
    ```

    The watch exits 0 scored, 3 errored, 4 still pending at the limit, 5 not listed, and the wake brings that code;
    the deadline goes a little past the limit. Where no ref is known, `--message <text>` takes the newest
    submission with exactly that description and names its ref and date in the result line: give every submission
    its own description. A poller that runs now ends as it is. After an unclear submit, read
    the submissions with `--page-size 100`, then `kaggle_submit.py <record.json> --landed <ref>` when the row is
    there, `--checked` when a clean read shows none: a script that passed `--checked` after a failed or empty read
    is wrong, since such a read decides nothing.
12. **What happens by itself, and what it costs.** Let each happen now, in this turn:
    - **Each `.venv-kaggle` is rebuilt once,** at the next gateway call, because the gateway now installs a pinned
      `six` beside the pinned CLI: about a second on a warm uv cache, a download on a cold one. Run `python3
      <pack>/plugins/kaggle/tools/kaggle.py --version` once. Every context has its own venv (the project root, each
      task folder), and each is rebuilt at its own next call.
    - **The leaderboard index is upgraded once,** at the first read of the store: each snapshot is read for the new
      flag and `index.jsonl` is rewritten, in seconds (about 8 for 1,800 snapshots). Run `python3
      <pack>/plugins/kaggle/tools/kaggle_lb.py summary <slug>` once.
    - **Snapshots in which Kaggle served a page again are passed by as unreliable.** A new read is unreliable
      when a whole page repeated, 3 or more rows came twice or far too many pages were read; one or two rows that
      came twice are the board moving, and that read stays a board (`"moved"`). A saved snapshot counts as
      unreliable when it holds any duplicate row, about one saved walk in a hundred, so a rank, a mover or a chart
      point of the past now and then comes from the reliable snapshot before it. `summary` counts them. Nothing is
      deleted.
    - **The status page's AI spend comes from autocode:** today's spend and the total of the master's agent, as
      `autocode me --json` gives them in the session that builds the page. Autocode keeps no per-day history, so
      the agent's last 7 days read n/a and the chart shows the cost ledger alone.
    - **The hour-close builds the page offline.** It takes the day's slots, the latest and the best submission and
      the GPU week from the newest hourly receipt, when that pass is under two hours old, and names the read's
      time. After a submission or a score, build the page once with its Kaggle reads (step 6's `flock` line).
    - **The first check has no news to compare:** its plain forum and notebooks flags decide, and the older
      submissions that come into view with the listing of 100 rows are no news.
13. **What stays the project's own:** its host, queue and job checks (beside the plugin's check, step 7); its
    preflight after a restart; its submission records and any gate specific to the competition; the content of its
    review packages (`prompt.md`, `STATE.md`) and what it does with the findings; its wakes beyond the standard
    set; its `L` directions; its tools under `tools/`. The plugin replaces only the hourly Kaggle reads and what
    wakes for them, the review runner, the job files and the score watch.
14. **Close.** Rewrite `<pack>/.memory/context.md` (the wakes with their checks, the jobs, what is left of this
    list), commit the pack's changes by the project's own rules, and send the owner one report: the wakes as set,
    the hosts named, the settings chosen, the small review's cost, and anything left open.

## Validate

- At the Solaris root, `uv run -m solaris.tools.plugins check --dir projects/<slug>` exits 0.
- `autocode_wake action=show name=hourly` shows the guard as the check (`sh
  <pack>/plugins/kaggle/tools/kaggle_hourly_check.sh <slug>`) and its `check_timeout`. Run that command once in a
  turn: it prints `quiet: <slug> <slot>: ...` (exit 1) or `HOURLY WAKE <slug> <slot>: ...` (exit 0) and names a
  receipt under `__data/kaggle/<slug>/hourly/`. Run it again in the same hour: the same result, and no Kaggle
  read. Then `--close` prints `page: rebuilt offline in <n> s (the build's output: ...)`, `receipt: ... marked
  handled`, the list `closed with it, in full` and the block headed `STATUS as of <the pass's time>`. After a few
  hours, `autocode_wake action=list` shows runs of `hourly` that its check skipped.
- `__data/kaggle/<slug>/presubmit/config.json` names the hosts, and a quiet line of the check no longer says that
  comments were held with the host mark unknown without hosts to tell them by.
- The plain pass's `agents` line reads `master <id> on <model> ...; workers: not read` with no flag, and its
  `spend` line starts with `autocode $<x> today`.
- `python3 <pack>/plugins/kaggle/tools/kaggle.py --version` prints the pinned CLI's version with no `installing`
  line the second time.
- `python3 <pack>/plugins/kaggle/tools/kaggle_lb.py summary <slug>` runs, and counts the unreliable snapshots when
  there are some.
- `kaggle_review.py --gate --model <provider/model>` exits 0 for each reviewer; the small review wrote
  `__out/review/<ID>/review.md` and one line in `<pack>/.memory/spend.jsonl`.
- `python3 <pack>/plugins/kaggle/tools/kaggle_jobs.py list` names each review job's file.
- The pre-flight grep finds no `session_clock`, `cap 55`, `ai_spend` or warm-cache line and no `ODnn` minted by the
  project in `<pack>/instructions.md`, `<pack>/card.md` and `tools/`, and no poll script waits for a score.

## Revert

- First, while the 0.11.0 tools are there: `python3 <pack>/plugins/autocode/tools/guard.py job list`. A watch that
  still runs ends by itself and fires its wake; wait for it, or stop it (`guard.py job stop <job>`).
- Set the `hourly` wake again without the guard: 0.10.0 has neither `kaggle_hourly_check.sh` nor `--check`, so
  the wake goes back to a turn every hour that runs the plain pass, or to the check script the project had before.
- The owner, or the session that manages the update, restores the 0.10.0 copy and the manifest entry `{"name":
  "kaggle", "version": "0.10.0"}` from the plugin's git history, with one exception: **keep 0.11.0's
  `tools/kaggle.py`**. The gateway of 0.10.0 builds its venv without `six`, and on a venv built today every CLI call
  then fails with `ModuleNotFoundError`.
- The state added here can stay: 0.10.0 ignores the receipts, the locks, the build log, the watch and review
  folders and `<pack>/jobs/`, and reads `last.json` and the leaderboard index as they are. It reads a snapshot
  marked unreliable as an ordinary one, as it did before, and its status page reads no spend from autocode.
  Autocode can stay attached, and the settings added here are ignored.
- The pack text of steps 4 and 5 is the project's own: keep the `L` numbers, and put a session clock line back only
  where the master goes back to a harness that runs one.
