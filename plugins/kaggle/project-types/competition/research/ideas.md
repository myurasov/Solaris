The ranked backlog of ideas for {{NAME}}. Ideas come from the owner, the day-0 survey, the forum, public notebooks,
past winners' writeups, the top-teams research track and our own results; each gets a feasibility check before it
takes compute or a submission slot.

## How This File Works

- **Add** an idea as a row: the next id (`I-01`, `I-02`, ...), one line, its source (a link, a topic id, a
  notebook, a run or a score), the date, the status `new`, and a rank.
- **Status:** `new` -> `researching` -> `feasible` or `not feasible` -> `planned` -> `tried` or `dropped`. A `tried`
  idea names its run or submission and the result; a `dropped` one says why.
- **Rank** by expected usefulness for the current phase goal (1 is the most useful), and re-rank as evidence arrives
  (a holdout read, a prototype, a score).
- **Go criterion:** fixed before the experiment runs, with a stop rule for a cheap early exit (a one-day probe
  before a long retrain). An idea reaches a submission only after an honest experiment against its criterion.
- **Depth:** keep at least five `new` ideas; run an idea-generation pass when fewer remain.
- **Top-teams track:** each idea it brings is recorded as taken, parked or rejected, with the reason.
- **Feasibility notes** go below, one heading per idea: gain, cost, risk, platform limits, licences, sources, next
  step.
